# Egyptian broiler vaccination reference

This application provides a **veterinarian-configurable selection reference**, not a universal immunization schedule or a claim that every commercial product is registered or appropriate for every farm. Vaccine use, age, route, strain, product availability, withdrawal guidance, and official requirements must be determined by the farm veterinarian and the local competent authority. Only healthy birds should be vaccinated and the farm should follow product labeling and cold-chain instructions.

| Category in the application | Broiler disease target | Reference basis | Display treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core reference | Marek’s disease | The Egyptian Health Council (EHC) broiler table lists a day-one subcutaneous option. [1] | Shown as a veterinarian-planned day-one option. |
| Core reference | Newcastle disease (ND) | The EHC broiler table lists day-one and 14–21 day options. [1] | Shown as a selectable disease target; no automatic date is imposed. |
| Core reference | Infectious bronchitis (IB) | The EHC broiler table lists an IB option for 14–21 days; MSD Animal Health Egypt also lists an Egypt-market combined IB/Gumboro/ND product for chickens. [1] [2] | Shown as a selectable disease target, including a combination label. |
| Core reference | Infectious bursal disease (IBD/Gumboro) | The EHC broiler table lists day-one and 14–21 day options; MSD Animal Health Egypt identifies Gumboro as an antigen in a local combined product. [1] [2] | Shown as a selectable disease target, including a combination label. |
| Local-program option | Avian influenza H5/H9 | Egyptian broiler surveillance literature identifies H5 and H9 together with ND and IB as important respiratory viral pathogens. [3] | Shown only under “additional programs”; the UI requires veterinary/authority confirmation. |
| Local-program option | ILT, avian reovirus, coccidiosis | These programs depend on farm history, production system, product availability, and veterinary decision. | Available only as veterinary-selected options, with no embedded schedule. |
