# User-authorized disposable sale lifecycle verification

The user approved a temporary sale workflow against an existing Ahmed trader account. A fixed session was created for 10 planned cages, with five cages per batch, 10 birds per cage, a 10 kg tare sample across five cages, a price of 70 EGP/kg, and a 50 EGP discount. The reference and notes explicitly identify the record as temporary verification data.

The first five-cage batch was recorded with 160 kg gross weight, then edited to 162 kg gross weight. The view updated from 150 kg to 152 kg net, from 10,500 EGP to 10,640 EGP gross value, and from 10,450 EGP to 10,590 EGP net due after the fixed discount. The activity log recorded both creation and edit events.

A second five-cage temporary batch was recorded at 158 kg gross weight, producing 148 kg net weight. The sale reached 10 of 10 planned cages, 300 kg net weight, 21,000 EGP gross value, and 20,950 EGP net due after the discount. The first and second batches were then reordered; the table now shows the 148 kg batch as sequence one and the 152 kg batch as sequence two, with an audit entry for the reorder.
