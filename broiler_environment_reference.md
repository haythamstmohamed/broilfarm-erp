# Broiler environmental target reference

The dashboard uses the following conservative operational targets at bird level. They are **planning references** and must be adjusted by a veterinarian or poultry-production specialist for housing system, ventilation, air speed, litter condition, breed, bird behaviour, and local climate.

| Cycle age | House-temperature target | Relative-humidity target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Days 1–7 | 32–34°C decreasing to 30–32°C | 50–60% |
| Days 8–14 | 26–28°C | 45–60% |
| Days 15–21 | 22–24°C | 45–60% |
| Days 22+ | 20–22°C | 50–60% (keep below 65% where practical) |

## Sources

1. [PoultryHub, *Climate in Poultry Houses*](https://www.poultryhub.org/all-about-poultry/husbandry-management/climate-in-poultry-houses): gives broiler temperature stages of 32–34°C on the first day, declining to 30°C in week 1, 26°C in week 2, 22°C in week 3, and 20°C in week 4; it describes 60–80% as an ideal broad humidity range and advises evaluating conditions at bird level.
2. [University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences, *Broiler Management*](https://www.uvas.edu.pk/advisory_service/PAS/comercial/Broiler.php): provides early-age temperature and humidity guidance and lists 45–65% relative humidity within its air-quality guidance.
3. [aviNews, Cobb-Vantress specialist guidance](https://avinews.com/en/cobb-management-strategies-to-broiler-performance/): states that house relative humidity should remain below 65% and emphasizes bird behaviour and bird-level conditions.

## Implementation verification

In the preview, selecting the placement date (cycle day 1) displayed a temperature target of 32–34°C and a relative-humidity target of 50–60%. Selecting 24 August 2026 (cycle day 7) updated the displayed target to 28–30°C while retaining 50–60% relative humidity. The same selected date also updated the dashboard age, weather context, feed/water forecast, and expected live-weight reference.
